MathLings Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

MathLings is a local Doomlings scoring helper. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

No accounts

MathLings does not require a login, password, or account.

Photos and camera

Camera and photo library access are used only to read Doomlings card faces for on-device scoring help. Photos stay on your device. MathLings does not upload photos, player names, or scores to our servers.

Session data

Player names, trait piles, and scores stay in memory for the current scoring session. Starting a new game clears that session.

Permissions

Android may show Camera (and Photos, when you pick from the library) permission prompts. Microphone / audio recording is not used for scoring.

Third parties

MathLings does not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs that track you across apps. Distribution through Google Play is subject to Google’s own policies.

Children

MathLings is a tabletop companion app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
